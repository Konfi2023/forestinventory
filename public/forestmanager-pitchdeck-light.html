The Problem

Europe's forests are managed blind.

16 million private forest owners manage 100+ million hectares with paper, Excel, and gut feeling. No digital infrastructure exists to collect, connect, or analyze forest data at scale.

1
No unified tool

Forest owners juggle 6–10 disconnected tools: GIS software, paper forms, weather apps, Excel sheets, government portals. Nothing integrates.

2
No data infrastructure

Ground-truth data — tree measurements, soil conditions, health status — exists only in paper folders or a forester's head. Never digitized, never analyzed.

3
Regulation forces action — now

EUDR (Jan 2027) requires digital traceability. CSRD demands nature impact reporting. No digital forest = no compliance.
