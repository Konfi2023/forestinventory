The Problem

Two problems. One root cause.

Europe's forests are not digitized. That creates an immediate crisis for millions of owners — and a systemic blind spot for everyone who depends on forest data.

The Acute Problem

Forest owners are stuck in the analog age.

16 million private owners manage 100+ million hectares with paper maps, Excel, and phone calls. There is no affordable, unified digital tool.

✗ 6–10 disconnected tools per owner
✗ No mobile, no offline, no integration
✗ EUDR (Jan 2027) demands digital traceability — now

The Structural Problem

Nobody understands Europe's forests in real time.

National forest inventories are a decade old. Insurers, researchers, carbon markets, policymakers — all work with estimates and outdated samples. There is no live data infrastructure.

✗ No real-time forest health data at scale
✗ CO₂ estimates based on generic models, not measurements
✗ Satellites see canopies, not individual trees
